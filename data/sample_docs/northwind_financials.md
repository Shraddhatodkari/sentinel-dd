# Financial Performance

Northwind Logistics reported audited revenue of USD 180 million for FY2026, a revenue growth of 12 percent year on year. EBITDA margin improved to 14 percent. The debt to equity ratio stands at 0.6 and all statutory filings were submitted on time with zero late filings in the last five years.

# Auditor Opinion

The statutory auditor issued an unqualified (clean) audit opinion for FY2026 with no going-concern qualifications.
