# Identity

BlueOrbit Analytics Inc is incorporated in United States. The company was founded in 2017 and provides cloud data analytics services to financial institutions.

# Ownership

BlueOrbit Analytics Inc is a wholly owned subsidiary of Orbit Capital Partners LLC. Orbit Capital Partners LLC is incorporated in Delaware. Daniel Brooks holds 70% of Orbit Capital Partners LLC.

# Officers

Daniel Brooks serves as CEO of BlueOrbit Analytics Inc. Anita Verma serves as director of BlueOrbit Analytics Inc. Anita Verma is a politically exposed person who previously served as a deputy minister of commerce. BlueOrbit Analytics Inc has a service agreement with Helios Trade FZE.
