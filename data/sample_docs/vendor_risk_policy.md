# Scope

This policy governs onboarding and periodic review of all third-party vendors with access to customer data or payment flows.

# Risk Tiers

Vendors with a risk score of 0.7 or above are classified as high risk and require approval by the Third-Party Risk Committee. Vendors with a risk score between 0.4 and 0.7 are classified as medium risk and require reviewer sign-off. Any potential sanctions match must be adjudicated by a human compliance officer before onboarding.

# Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced due diligence is mandatory when a vendor director or beneficial owner is a politically exposed person, when ownership includes more than one offshore holding layer, or when adverse media alleges fraud or money laundering.
