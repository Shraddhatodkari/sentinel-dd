# Certifications

BlueOrbit Analytics holds ISO 27001 certification and completed a SOC 2 Type II audit in 2026 with two minor exceptions related to access reviews.

# Security Incident

BlueOrbit Analytics suffered a data breach in March 2025 exposing 12,000 customer records through a misconfigured cloud storage bucket. The company notified regulators within 72 hours and paid a regulatory fine of USD 250,000.
