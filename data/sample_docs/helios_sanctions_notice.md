# Designation

Viktor Rask is listed on the Global Consolidated sanctions list following a 2026 designation for facilitating the diversion of dual-use goods. Assets controlled by Viktor Rask are subject to freezing measures and dealings with entities he owns or controls require enhanced scrutiny.
